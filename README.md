# Fiber Art Scroll

An endless, Instagram-style scroll of art, about 90% fiber art and 10% painting, mixed with posts on techniques, artists, fiber colors, tools and history. Each post can be journaled about privately (saved only on the viewer's own device) and shared.

Live app: https://fiberartscroll.netlify.app

- `index.html`: the app
- `art.json`: all posts
- `CONTENT_GUIDE.md`: how new posts are written
- `tools/merge.js`: adds a batch of new posts to `art.json`

New posts are added daily and published automatically by Netlify.
